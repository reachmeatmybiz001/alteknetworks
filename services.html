NETWORKING

Networking Solutions

Switching

Local network (LAN) switches form the core for all networks, providing high-speed connectivity and communication systems. Apart from efficiently and securely transmitting data, networks should also support new services, stay in sync with evolving traffic patterns and ensure optimization of application performance.

From core to edge, a good network switch provides performance, high availability, comprehensive security, delivery optimization and enhanced manageability, helping organizations optimize their network infrastructure.

Modular Switches

Modular network switches provide simplified operations, media flexibility and expandability to extend deployment life and minimize costs. These switches provide integrated security, high availability, strong support for converged applications and investment protection.

Fixed Configuration Switches

Fixed configuration switches provide Ethernet switching solutions for enterprise branches, offices, campuses and small and medium-sized businesses. They provide access security, sustainability, operational excellence and enhanced work experience while helping optimize power consumption.

Wireless

Meet the demands of your wireless network with fast, reliable Wi-Fi performance in mobile environments. Our Access Points and Controllers provide an easy way to scale your wireless network while maintaining security and performance.

CYBER SECURITY

Security Solutions

Next-Generation Firewalls

Security vulnerabilities are no longer bound by enterprise walls. Protecting your digital workplace and gaining visibility and control to respond to attacks is critical to the security of your business.

Our security portfolio helps you manage the ever-changing threat landscape and prepare your organization to proactively deal with cyber attacks.

Gateway Protection

Gateway protection refers to security appliances used for centralizing gateway-level protection for incoming and outgoing network traffic while controlling applications and bandwidth without impacting performance and scalability.

Remote Site Protection

Security systems incorporate Virtual Private Networks (VPNs) across multiple locations and branch offices. VPN technology helps prevent vulnerabilities and malicious code by filtering infected traffic before it can access the network, in real time without user intervention.

Unified Threat Management (UTM) Appliance

Unified Threat Management delivers an all-in-one network protection solution by bringing together gateway anti-virus, anti-spyware, intrusion prevention, content filtering, firmware updates and 24x7 support onto a high-performance platform.

UTM solutions are automatically updated with signatures that stop attacks before they enter the network, ensuring round-the-clock protection. UTM provides network security against threats including viruses, spyware, worms and Trojans while remaining simple to install and manage.

Endpoint Protection

Quickly find and remediate attacks on endpoints so productivity is not compromised, with automation and centrally managed visibility into threats.

Endpoint security protects entry points such as desktops, laptops and mobile devices from being exploited by malicious actors and campaigns, whether endpoints are on a network or in the cloud.
